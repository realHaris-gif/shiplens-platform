About ShipLens Organization

Building resilient, transparent community infrastructure through technology-driven accountability.

Our Mission

ShipLens was founded to eliminate operational friction and financial ambiguity in distributed non-profit networks. By transitioning from vulnerable paper receipts and spreadsheets to cryptographic audit trails, we ensure that every rupee collected in the field directly serves its intended civic purpose.

Governance Structure

Our organization is structured across semi-autonomous regional branches supervised by an executive Head Office. Regional leads collect dues and run field activities, while centralized Finance Reviewers audit weekly submissions before funds are remitted to main accounts.
